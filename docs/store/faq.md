# Thulir MicroGreen Tracker: Frequently Asked Questions

## Getting started

**Do I need an account or internet connection?**
No. There is no sign-up or login, and the app works fully offline. Everything is saved on your phone.

**What is a batch, and what is a tray?**
A batch is one crop sown on one day, for example "Radish sown 5 October". A batch holds one or more trays. Each batch
gets a number (B001, B002, …) and each tray gets its own number (T001, T002, …). The stage, watering, photos and notes
belong to the batch; each tray has its own position, lost status and harvest weight.

**How do I add a batch with several trays?**
Tap **New batch** on the Home tab (or **+** on the Batches tab), choose the crop, and set "Number of trays". Tray numbers and positions are filled in
automatically; tap "Change" to pick different positions. Enter the seed weight per tray (it applies to every tray in
the batch), then tap "Add N trays".

**What are tray positions?**
Positions are the spots on your racks where a tray can sit, for example Rack A / Shelf 2. The app hands out free
positions to new trays and frees them again when a batch is harvested or a tray is lost. How many positions you have
comes from your rack layout under Settings → Racks & shelves.

**How do I set up my racks and shelves?**
Go to Settings → Racks & shelves and enter how many racks you have, the shelves per rack and the trays per shelf, then
tap "Save rack layout". The Shelves tab then draws your racks, and each tray shows where it sits (for example Rack A /
Shelf 2, plus the slot when a shelf holds more than one tray). Tap a tray to open its own page with its batch, photos and harvest.

## Numbering

**How are batch and tray numbers chosen?**
They count up from the last number used and are never reused, even if you delete a batch. You can see and change the
next numbers under Settings → Numbering, for example to continue from labels you already use on paper.

**Do numbers continue after restoring a backup or moving to a new phone?**
Yes. The last numbers are saved with your data, so after a restore the next batch and tray carry on where the backup
left off.

## Growing

**How do I move a batch to the next stage?**
Open the batch on the Batches tab and tap "Mark as Germination", "Mark as Growing" and so on. The stages are Sowing,
Germination, Growing, Ready to Harvest and Completed.

**How do I record watering?**
Tap "Water" on the batch. The amount is optional and can be in ml, cups, liters or sprays.

**How do I add notes?**
Tap "Note" on the batch, choose a type (general, observation, watering, fertilizer or issue) and write your note.
Notes appear on the batch card, newest first.

**Can I photograph individual trays?**
Yes. In "Add Photo", choose the tray under "Which tray?" (or "Whole batch") and the stage the photo shows. The batch
gallery can then be filtered by tray and by stage, and photos can be compared side by side.

## Losses and harvest

**A tray failed. How do I record it?**
Open the batch and tap "Report lost trays". Pick the trays, a reason (fungus or mould, pest attack, physical damage and
more), the date and an optional note. Lost trays free their positions and appear in Settings → Insights & reports → Tray Losses.

**I marked a tray lost by mistake. Can I undo it?**
Yes. Tap "Edit batch & trays" and tap "Growing again" next to the tray.

**Can I add my own loss reasons?**
Yes, under Settings → Loss reasons.

**How do I record the harvest?**
When a batch is "Ready to Harvest", tap "Harvest & weigh trays", enter the weight of each tray in grams (or use "Fill
all") and tap "Harvest". You can correct weights later with "Edit tray weights".

**Can I export a report?**
Yes. Open Settings → Insights & reports, choose a crop (or all) and the sowing dates, then tap "PDF report" or "Export CSV". The
file opens in your phone's share sheet, so you can save it or send it by email.

**What is the seed-to-yield ratio?**
Grams harvested divided by grams of seed sown. For example 635 g harvested from 75 g of seed is 8.5×. It appears on
the batch card and in Reports when the seed weight was entered.

## Supplies

**How do I keep track of my seed and coco peat?**
Open the Supplies tab and add each seed (for a crop) and your coco peat, with how much you bought and, if you like, the
price, supplier and expiry date. Every new batch then takes its seed (trays × seed per tray) and its coco peat (trays ÷
trays per block) off the stock, oldest stock first. The new-batch form shows how much seed is left before you save.

**How does the app warn me when something runs low?**
Each supply has a "warn me at" amount. When the stock falls to it, or a purchase is close to its expiry date, it shows at
the top of Supplies and in Today's care on Home.

**Can the app work out what a batch cost?**
Yes, when you enter the price you paid for your seed and coco peat. The batch card then shows what its supplies cost,
in total and per tray.

**How does stock work?**
When you weigh a harvest, its grams go into Shop → Stock with a use-by date from the crop's shelf life (set it for each
crop under Settings → Crops & categories; 7 days if not set). Stock close to its use-by date shows under Today and at
the top of Stock. Open a crop to record waste or correct what's left; you can also add stock by hand.

**How do I record a sale?**
In Shop, tap "New sale" (on Today, or Orders → Sales). Choose the customer, or leave it as a walk-in, add each crop and its
grams, and the price is worked out from Settings → Price list (per 100 g, optionally different for restaurants, shops
or homes). The grams come off your stock, earliest use-by first. If they didn't pay in full, turn off "Paid in full"
and enter what they paid; the rest shows as owed under Today and on the customer.

**How do I record a customer's payment?**
Open the customer and use "Record a payment": it pays off their oldest sales first. You can also record a payment on a
single sale, and share the bill on WhatsApp or any app from the sale.

**How do I take an order for a later day?**
In Shop → Orders, tap "New order", choose the customer, the delivery date and what they want. On that day it shows
under Today → Deliveries. Tap "Deliver" to record the sale: the order's crops and grams are filled in and priced from
your price list, and you can change them if you deliver a different amount. Orders you haven't delivered stay on the
list as overdue until you deliver or cancel them.

**How do subscriptions work?**
A subscription is a delivery that repeats: choose the customer, what they get, the days of the week, and whether it's
every week or every other week. Each delivery day shows under Today, ready to deliver. On the subscription you can skip
a single day, pause it until a date (for a holiday), end it, or change it. Restaurants and shops are set to pay later
when you deliver; homes are set to paid in full. You can change either before saving.

**What's a mixed box?**
Most subscriptions are a mix rather than set crops. Choose "Mixed box", the box's weight and how many crops go in it
(for example 300 g of 3), and, if the customer has favourites, the crops it may use. For each delivery the app picks
from what's ready, the crops to use first and different from the customer's last box, and Today shows what to pack.
Choose "Chosen crops" instead for a customer who always gets the same.

**How does the app plan for my subscriptions?**
Every upcoming delivery shows where its microgreens will come from: "Ready from stock", a growing batch ("Radish from
B012, ready Sat, Oct 24"), a sowing still to make ("sow Pea Shoots by Thu, Oct 15"), or what's short. Mixed boxes rotate
through your crops week to week, using the ones that can grow in time. The sowings appear on Farm Home under "Sow for
orders".

**Does the app keep a record of each delivery?**
Yes. Each subscription has a delivery log for the last 8 weeks: delivered (tap it to see the sale), skipped, paused or
missed. A delivery day that passes without a delivery stays on Today as overdue for a week, with "Deliver" (if you
delivered late) and "Missed". After a week it shows in the log as not recorded.

**Can I send the day's delivery list to someone?**
Yes. Under Today → Deliveries, tap "Share delivery list" to send the names, phone numbers, addresses and what each
customer gets to WhatsApp or any app. If you don't have enough stock for the day's deliveries, Today tells you which
crop is short.

**I deleted a delivery's sale by mistake. What happens?**
Its microgreens go back into stock and the delivery goes back on the list, so you can deliver it again.

**How does the app know what to sow for my orders?**
When Shop is on, Farm Home shows "Sow for orders". It looks at your orders and subscriptions for the next few weeks,
takes off what's in stock and what your growing batches should give (by their expected harvest date), and suggests
how many trays to sow, and by when, for the rest. Trays are worked out from the average weight per tray of your last
weighed harvests of that crop (150 g a tray until you've weighed one). Tap a suggestion to start the batch with the
crop and trays filled in. If a delivery is too soon to grow for, it says so, and it warns you when you don't have
enough free tray positions for the week's sowings.

**Can the app remind me what to do each day?**
Yes. Turn on Settings → Notifications. Each morning (7:00 AM unless you change it) you get a summary of the day:
batches to water, deliveries (including overdue ones), sowings for your orders, batches to harvest and stock to sell
soon. In the evening (6:00 PM) there's a reminder if something is still to do. Choose which of these to include.
Reminders are set for the coming week each time you open the app, so open it now and then to keep them up to date.

**Where can I see my profit?**
In Shop, tap "Profit report" on Today (or Settings → Profit report). For this month, last month, the last 30 days or
this year it shows your sales, the growing cost (the seed and growing media each harvested batch used, from the prices
you entered in Supplies), the profit, what was collected and what's still owed, and the same by crop and for your top
customers. "Download sales (CSV)" saves the period's sales for a spreadsheet.

**What's the Farm / Shop switch at the top?**
Farm is for growing: trays, batches and supplies. Shop is for selling: orders, subscriptions, sales, customers, stock
and what's owed. If you don't sell, turn Shop off under Settings → Shop;
you can also pick Shop's colour and your currency there.

## Crops and categories

**Can I add crops that aren't in the list?**
Yes. Go to Settings → Crops & categories, tap Add Crop Type and enter the days to germination and harvest, watering frequency and light hours.

**Can I create my own categories and icons?**
Yes. Under Settings → Crops & categories you can add, rename and delete categories and tap the icon to choose a new one.
"Add standard microgreens" adds any of the built-in crops and categories you don't have yet without changing yours.

## Backups and new phones

**Is my data backed up?**
The app saves a copy on your phone automatically every day (Settings → Backup & restore → Restore lists them). For protection against
losing or replacing your phone, use Settings → Backup & restore → Save File regularly and save the file to Google Drive, Files or email.

**How do I move to a new phone?**
On the old phone tap Settings → Backup & restore → Save File and save it somewhere you can reach from the new phone (for example Google
Drive). On the new phone install the app, tap "Restore from a backup file" (or Settings → Backup & restore → Restore File) and choose the
file. Your batches, photos, settings and numbering come back.

**What happens if I uninstall the app?**
All its data is removed from the phone. Save a backup file first if you want to keep it.

**Can two phones share the same data?**
Not automatically; the data lives on one phone. You can move it with a backup file, but changes are not synced
between phones.

## Privacy

**Do you see my data?**
No. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Data only leaves your phone when you share a backup file or a photo yourself.
See the privacy policy: https://thulirmicrogreentracker.github.io/Microgreentracker/store/privacy-policy

**Why does the app ask for the camera?**
Only to take photos of your trays when you tap "Take photo".
