# Delete your Thulir MicroGreen Tracker account

- **App:** Thulir MicroGreen Tracker (Android and iOS)
- **Developer:** Rajeshkumar
- **Contact:** thulirmicrogreentracker@gmail.com

Signing in to Thulir MicroGreen Tracker is optional. If you created an account, you can delete it at any time.

## In the app

1. Open Thulir MicroGreen Tracker.
2. Tap **Config** at the bottom, then go to **Account**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

The account is deleted straight away. (If the app asks you to sign in again first, that is a security check: sign
out, sign in, and tap Delete account again.)

## Without the app

Email **thulirmicrogreentracker@gmail.com** from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my
account". We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in account: name, email address, user ID and sign-in method.
- Your name and email in our subscription service (RevenueCat).

## What is kept

- **Your batches, trays and photos on your phone.** These were never uploaded, so deleting the account does not
  touch them. Uninstall the app to remove them.
- **Purchase records** for a subscription or lifetime purchase, which the store and RevenueCat keep for the purchase
  to keep working (use Restore purchases) and for tax and accounting. Email us if you want them deleted too.
- **The free-trial record:** the trial start date, stored under a one-way fingerprint of your email. It contains no
  email address or other details and is kept so that the free trial is not started again.
